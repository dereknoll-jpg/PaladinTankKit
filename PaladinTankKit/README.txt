Paladin TankKit 1.0.1 - WoW Forever (Interface 16001)
Mark. Track. Protect.

/ptk opens the new settings panel: Overview, Ability timers, Dungeon marking,
and Appearance. The AddOns list and settings use the gold shield-and-hammer logo.

MARKING: Select a living elite in a dungeon party to begin. Mouseover only scouts;
it cannot start automatic marking. Before combat, only the selected elite is seeded.
Other eligible elites join with readable party engagement. Dangerous enemies and
casters can take opening priority; then Skull remains stable until confirmed death,
with selected boss-add exceptions. Cross promotes after confirmed death.
Requires leader/assistant. Never auto marks raids, world bosses or outdoor encounters.
Non-elites are never marked. Manual pins and crowd-control markers are respected.
WAIT = select an elite; AUTO = armed queue; PAUSE = attention needed; MAN = manual.

TIMERS: Active Seal, Consecration, Hammer of Justice, Holy Shield and talented
Templar's Bulwark share one rail. Individual switches control their visibility.
Seals and Holy Shield use actual buff expiration. Judgement does not clear or reset
them. Consecration uses an adjustable ground-effect estimate (default 8 seconds).
Version 1.0.1 adds direct player aura lookups by name/ID. Seal procs cannot clear
or restart a buff timer. Target changes do not invalidate player buff snapshots.
Consecration prefers a readable buff or ground-effect slot and can recover a missed
cast from its cooldown start. Displayed timer artwork stays full-colour and opaque.
Cooldowns use Blizzard's readable data, including recovery after reloads/zoning/death.
A delayed initial cooldown update is not treated as Ready. Unused spells stay hidden.
Confirmed cooldown completion briefly lights up, then clears. Forbearance is a badge.
~ means estimated timing. ? means unavailable timing. Hidden data can limit tracking.

REMINDERS: Optional Righteous Fury, no selected Paladin aura, and equipped shield
at 20% durability or below. Missing Seals never trigger reminders. No shield, offhand
weapons, held items and bagged shields never trigger a shield durability warning.

NOTES: 28 instance catalogs, 193 selected boss notes, 58 named priority entries.
Notes open in a single-line ribbon. Hover for full text. Share is explicit and limited
to once per 15 seconds. Reference/limited-confidence guidance is labeled.

/ptk preview demonstrates the rail and sample notes outside combat. Preview notes
cannot send party chat and preview never marks enemies. Reduced Motion is available.
/ptk help lists commands. Bind Mark Pull, Skull, Cross and Clear in WoW Key Bindings.

Coverage is selected, not a full dungeon journal. Some identities use English names.
See SOURCES.md and COVERAGE.md for research, confidence and limitations.
