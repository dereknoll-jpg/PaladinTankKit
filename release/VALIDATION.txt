Version 1.0.5 local validation

921 automated behavioral checks pass, plus Lua 5.1 syntax, TOC/bindings,
initialization, class isolation and texture checks. This includes 70 protection /
personal cleanse checks, 58 Forever encounter checks and 134 hide/restore checks.

Protection tests cover concurrent buffs, independent observed deadlines, readable
early removal, zero absorb, secret identities/expirations/amounts, bounded remembered
timing, death/mount suppression, full-colour artwork, fixed positions, Effects /
Reduced Motion, individual switches and middle-click hiding/restoration.

Personal cleanse is client-filtered, player-only and learned-spell-aware. Tests
cover Magic/Poison/Disease, Purify vs Cleanse, curses, Forbearance, missing spell
knowledge, hidden data, the master reminder switch and restoration.

Encounter tests cover private ribbon cues, dismissal/rearming, manual-plan retention,
observed casts/channels/adds/health crossings, target changes, dead/idle units,
version/instance restrictions, preview privacy and per-plan source/version metadata.
All twelve new plans fit explicit sharing. These modules never automatically send
chat, play alert sounds or change markers.

Native Forever validation of the new indicators and encounter cues has not yet
been performed. Actual client visibility, removable-aura filtering, icon loading
and animation pacing need in-game review. Refreshed plans are researched, not
live-tested; older manual entries have not been re-certified by this update.
