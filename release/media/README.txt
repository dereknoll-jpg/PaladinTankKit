Captured November 19, 2026 in WoW Forever, version 1.0.0. These are native client
captures, not generated interface mockups. Preview timers and the sample VanCleef
note are demonstrations, not footage from an actual dungeon encounter. Keep the
visible PREVIEW label and describe them as 'In-game preview mode'.

Only rectangular cropping and PNG/GIF conversion were applied. No spell art,
interface elements, lighting or gameplay were painted into the captures.
Settings crop: x1314 y398 width812 height652 from 3440x1440 sources.
HUD crop: x1214 y480 width920 height174 from the later windowed capture.
GIF: 64 frames, average source interval 322 ms, 320 ms playback interval,
infinite loop. The capture rate is lower than the client's animation rate.

Source and export scripts: scripts/export-release-media.ps1 and make-gif.ps1.
